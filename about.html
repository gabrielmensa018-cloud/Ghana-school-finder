WHO WE ARE

Helping students make informed choices.

Ghana School Finder is a school-discovery project designed to make it easier for students and families to explore educational options across Ghana.

Our mission

To organise useful school information in one accessible place so students can explore options and make thoughtful education decisions.

How it works

Search for a school, filter by region or school type, and open a school profile to view the information currently available. The directory will grow as verified records are added.

Student guide

When comparing schools, consider the programmes offered, location, day or boarding arrangements, learning environment and your own interests. Confirm current details with the school or the Ghana Education Service before making final decisions.

Education advertising

In a future version, schools and education organisations may be able to promote open days, approved events, learning services and scholarship opportunities. Advertisements should be clearly labelled and checked before publication.

Information and verification

This is an independent student project, not an official government website. For official school information, visit the Ghana Education Service and contact institutions directly.
